How to pay with Cash App

Tap the QR code or Open Cash App. Cash App opens with this invoice loaded, and you confirm the payment.

If nothing opens:

  1. Tap Copy invoice.
  2. Open Cash App and go to the Money tab.
  3. Tap Bitcoin, then Send.
  4. Scan the QR code or paste the invoice.
  5. Confirm with your PIN or Face ID.

You don't need to type the amount. The invoice expires when the timer runs out.
