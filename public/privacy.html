Last updated: [Effective date]. CPAY is operated by [Legal entity name], [Registered address].

Who is responsible

[Legal entity name] runs CPAY and decides how your personal data is used (the "controller"). Privacy contact: [Privacy contact email].

What CPAY does not have

CPAY does not keep balances for you (with instant forward it receives each payment only briefly to forward it) and never receives your wallet's recovery phrase, passphrase or private keys. If you use the CPAY Wallet app, its recovery phrase stays on your device, encrypted with your passphrase. We do not receive payers' card details, payment-account details or wallet balances.

What we collect and why

  • Account data: email address, display name, account type, business name if you give one, and the store details you choose to publish. Passwords are stored only as a hash by our authentication provider. Purpose: to provide your account (contract).
  • Sign-in security data: whether you set up two-step sign-in or passkeys, and times of sign-in attempts. Purpose: to protect your account (legitimate interest, security).
  • Receiving address: the Lightning address you save for payments and when it was checked. Purpose: to create invoices that pay you (contract).
  • Payment records: invoice amount, price reference, status, time, Lightning payment identifiers and proof of payment, and an approximate payer country and city from the network connection. Purpose: to show you your payments and investigate problems (contract, legitimate interest).
  • Abuse-prevention data: IP address and browser information, used for rate limits and bot checks, and an audit log of sensitive account and admin actions. Purpose: to prevent fraud and abuse (legitimate interest, legal obligation).

We do not use advertising or product-analytics trackers and we do not sell personal data. Your sign-in session is kept in your browser's local storage so you stay signed in.

Who processes data for us

  • Database, authentication and server functions: Supabase (servers in Singapore).
  • Website delivery, security and bot protection: Cloudflare.
  • Invoice creation and payment confirmation: the Lightning wallet provider behind the address you choose, which receives the invoice amount and description.
  • Price references: public exchange-rate sources (no personal data is sent).
  • Operator alerts: Telegram, for CPAY staff only; alerts contain payment amounts and the receiving account's display name.
  • Backups: an encrypted-at-rest private code repository on GitHub, containing payment and account records without email addresses.

Some of these providers process data outside your country. Where the law requires, we rely on appropriate safeguards such as standard contractual clauses.

How long we keep it

Account data: while your account is open and up to 12 months after closure. Payment and audit records: up to 6 years after the payment or action, or longer if the law requires. Security logs: up to 90 days. Backups are rotated on the same schedule.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you can ask to access, correct, delete or export your data, to restrict or object to some uses, and to withdraw consent where we rely on it. Write to [Privacy contact email]. We answer within one month. Some payment and audit records must be kept even after an account is closed. You may also complain to your data-protection authority.

Security

Data is encrypted in transit, access is limited to staff who need it, and administrators must use two-step sign-in. No system is perfectly secure; tell us about any concern at the address on the contact page.

Children

The Service is not for anyone under 18.

Changes

We post changes on this page and email account holders about material changes before they apply.
