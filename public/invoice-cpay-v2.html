How to pay

On this phone: tap Pay with Lightning or Pay with Cash App. Your wallet opens with the payment loaded and you confirm it.

On a computer: scan the QR code with your phone's wallet app.

If nothing opens:

  1. Tap Copy payment request.
  2. Open your wallet and choose Send or Scan.
  3. Paste the request, or scan the QR code.
  4. Check the amount and confirm.

You don't need to type the amount. The request expires when the timer runs out; you can generate a new one after that.
