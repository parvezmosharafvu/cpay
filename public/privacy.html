Who is responsible

[Legal entity name] runs CPAY and decides how your personal data is used (the "controller"). Privacy contact: [Privacy contact email].

What CPAY does not have

CPAY keeps a balance for you when Auto withdraw is off for your account; when it is on, CPAY receives each payment only to forward it. CPAY never receives your wallet's recovery phrase, passphrase or private keys. If you use the CPAY Wallet app, its recovery phrase stays on your device, encrypted with your passphrase. We do not receive payers' card details, payment-account details or wallet balances.

What we collect and why

  • Account data: email address, display name, account type, business name if you give one, and the store details you choose to publish. Passwords are stored only as a hash by our authentication provider. Purpose: to provide your account (contract).
  • Sign-in security data: whether you set up a passkey (your device keeps the private key; we keep only its public key), whether your email address is confirmed, and times of sign-in attempts. We do not offer sign-in with a social account. Purpose: to protect your account (legitimate interest, security).
  • Payout addresses: the Lightning address and any USDT addresses (with their network) you save, when they were added or checked, and which one payments are sent to. Purpose: to create invoices that pay you, or to forward your payments to you (contract).
  • Payment records: invoice amount, price reference, status, time, Lightning payment identifiers and proof of payment, and an approximate payer location (country, region and city) worked out on our servers from the network connection when the payment is created. Purpose: to show you your payments and investigate problems (contract, legitimate interest).
  • Location data: we only ever record approximate locations, never GPS or exact positions, and we do not use your device's location. This covers the payer location above; an optional location you set on your account (country, division or state, district or city, area); and the country your connection was in when you last opened your dashboard. Who sees it: the owner of a payment link sees the approximate payer location (country, region, city) of payments made on that link; you and CPAY admins see the location you set and your last-seen country; CPAY admins see all of these; payers do not see anyone's location. Purpose: to show you where your payments come from and to help us support users in their area (contract, legitimate interest). These locations are approximate and can be wrong, so we never use them to decide fees, limits or whether a payment is accepted. You can change or clear the location you set at any time in your profile; the last-seen country is updated automatically when you open your dashboard and cannot be cleared there.
  • Abuse-prevention data: your IP address, processed briefly and kept for rate limits only in a one-way hashed form, browser information used by bot checks, and an audit log of sensitive account and admin actions. Purpose: to prevent fraud and abuse (legitimate interest, legal obligation).

We do not use advertising or product-analytics trackers and we do not sell personal data. We use your browser's storage only for things the Service needs: your sign-in session (local storage) so you stay signed in, short-lived checkout and bot-check state (session storage), and, for admins, the last opened tab. Our bot-protection provider may set its own strictly necessary cookies.

Who processes data for us

  • Database, authentication and server functions: Supabase (servers in Singapore).
  • Website delivery, security and bot protection: Cloudflare.
  • Invoice creation and payment confirmation: for direct payments, the Lightning wallet provider behind the address you choose, which receives the invoice amount and description. For payments CPAY receives (Auto withdraw on or off), CPAY's own platform wallet creates and receives the invoice; when the amount is forwarded or withdrawn, it sends a separate payment to the provider behind the address or invoice you chose.
  • Price references: public exchange-rate sources (no personal data is sent).
  • Operator alerts: Telegram, for CPAY staff only; alerts contain payment amounts and the receiving account's display name.
  • Backups: an encrypted-at-rest private code repository on GitHub, containing payment and account records without email addresses.

Some of these providers process data outside your country. Where the law requires, we rely on appropriate safeguards such as standard contractual clauses.

How long we keep it

We aim to keep account data while your account is open and up to 12 months after closure; payment, forward and audit records up to 6 years after the payment or action, or longer if the law requires; and security logs up to 90 days. Some of these periods are applied by manual review rather than automatic deletion. Backups are rotated on a similar schedule.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you can ask to access, correct, delete or export your data, to restrict or object to some uses, and to withdraw consent where we rely on it. Write to [Privacy contact email]. We answer within one month. Some payment and audit records must be kept even after an account is closed. You may also complain to your data-protection authority.

Security

Data is encrypted in transit, access is limited to staff who need it, and administrators must sign in with a passkey. No system is perfectly secure; tell us about any concern at the address on the contact page.

Children

The Service is not for anyone under 18. Accounts are for adults only, and we do not knowingly collect data about children. If you believe a child has given us data, contact us and we will delete it where the law allows.

Changes

We post changes on this page and email account holders about material changes before they apply.
