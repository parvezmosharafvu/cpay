Refund and Dispute Policy

Last updated: [Effective date]. CPAY is operated by [Legal entity name], [Registered address].

How payments reach the merchant

When you pay through a CPAY link, your payment goes to the merchant: either directly to the merchant's own wallet, or briefly through CPAY, which deducts its disclosed fee and either forwards the rest to the payout address the merchant saved (Auto withdraw on) or adds it to the merchant's CPAY balance (Auto withdraw off). Lightning payments cannot be reversed, so refunds are made by the merchant.

Bitcoin and Lightning payments are final

Once a Lightning payment completes it cannot be reversed or charged back by CPAY, by the payer's wallet or by any network. Check the amount and the merchant name on the payment page before you pay.

Asking for a refund

  1. Contact the merchant you paid. The merchant is responsible for the goods or services they sell and for their own refund policy.
  2. If the merchant agrees, they send the refund from their own wallet to a Lightning invoice or address that you give them. A refund is a new payment; the amount in bitcoin may differ from the original if the price has changed, unless you and the merchant agree otherwise.
  3. If you cannot reach an agreement, report it through our complaints procedure with the payment link, the time you paid and, if you have it, the proof of payment from the receipt. CPAY can confirm whether the merchant's wallet confirmed the payment and can act under the Terms against an account used for fraud, but cannot make the refund itself.

Invoice problems

  • Expired invoice: if an invoice expires before it is paid, no money moves. Ask for a new link.
  • Paid but the page still waits: CPAY keeps checking with the wallet that issued the invoice (the merchant's wallet, or CPAY's platform wallet for forwarded payments) for several days and updates the receipt when proof arrives. Your own wallet's history shows whether you paid. Report it if it is not resolved.
  • Paid twice or overpaid: each invoice is for an exact amount. If you paid two invoices by mistake, ask the merchant to refund one; CPAY cannot.

Merchants

Merchants should publish their own refund terms to customers and handle refund requests promptly from their own wallets. The CPAY fee is deducted from each forwarded payment. If a merchant refunds a payer, the CPAY fee on the original payment is not returned, unless the problem was caused by CPAY. A forward that is delayed or held is not a refund: the amount stays owed to the merchant.

Recorded CPAY balances

For accounts that have a balance recorded by CPAY, withdrawals sent to a saved address or to a Lightning invoice the account holder provided cannot be recalled. A withdrawal that fails before it is sent returns to that balance.
