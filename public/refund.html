Refund and Dispute Policy

Last updated: [Effective date]. CPAY is operated by [Legal entity name], [Registered address].

Payments go to the merchant, not to CPAY

When you pay through a CPAY link, your payment goes to the merchant: either directly to the merchant's own wallet, or briefly through CPAY, which deducts its disclosed fee and immediately forwards the rest to the merchant's wallet. Lightning payments cannot be reversed, so refunds are made by the merchant.

Bitcoin and Lightning payments are final

Once a Lightning payment completes it cannot be reversed or charged back by CPAY, by the payer's wallet or by any network. Check the amount and the merchant name on the payment page before you pay.

Asking for a refund

  1. Contact the merchant you paid. The merchant is responsible for the goods or services they sell and for their own refund policy.
  2. If the merchant agrees, they send the refund from their own wallet to a Lightning invoice or address that you give them. A refund is a new payment; the amount in bitcoin may differ from the original if the price has changed, unless you and the merchant agree otherwise.
  3. If you cannot reach an agreement, report it through our complaints procedure with the payment link, the time you paid and, if you have it, the proof of payment from the receipt. CPAY can confirm whether the merchant's wallet confirmed the payment and can act under the Terms against an account used for fraud, but cannot make the refund itself.

Invoice problems

  • Expired invoice: if an invoice expires before it is paid, no money moves. Ask for a new link.
  • Paid but the page still waits: CPAY keeps checking with the merchant's wallet for several days and updates the receipt when proof arrives. Your own wallet's history shows whether you paid. Report it if it is not resolved.
  • Paid twice or overpaid: each invoice is for an exact amount. If you paid two invoices by mistake, ask the merchant to refund one; CPAY cannot.

Merchants

Merchants should publish their own refund terms to customers and handle refund requests promptly from their own wallets. CPAY fees, where they apply, are not refundable once the billing period has started, unless the problem was caused by CPAY.

Recorded CPAY balances

For accounts that have a balance recorded by CPAY, withdrawals sent to the address saved on the account cannot be recalled. A withdrawal that fails before it is sent returns to that balance.
