How it works

From a link to USDT in three steps

No storefront to build and no invoices to write by hand.

  1. Create a payment link

    Name it, pick a checkout design and choose who can pay: Cash App only, or Cash App and other Lightning wallets.

  2. Your customer pays

    They choose an amount and pay a standard Lightning invoice. The payment is credited to your balance once, when it settles.

  3. Withdraw USDT

    Save a USDT address on a supported network. Each quote shows the fee and the amount you receive before you send.

Features

Everything you need to get paid

Payment links

Each link opens a checkout with a Lightning invoice. The payer scans it and the payment lands on your balance.

Your own dashboard

See every payment, your balance and your withdrawals in one place, in US dollars.

Fees you can see

Every USDT withdrawal shows the fee and the amount you receive before you confirm it.

Ten checkout designs

Pick how payers see your payment page and invoice. Every design shows who is paid, the amount and the time left.

Accounts

For individuals and for businesses

Security

Protection around every payout

Two-step sign-in

Add an authenticator app or a passkey from the Security page of your desk.

Confirm before a payout

A withdrawal asks you to confirm it is you again when your sign-in is not recent.

Sign in again to change it

Changing where you get paid needs a fresh sign-in, and you get a notice every time it changes.

One credit, one send

A payment is credited once and a withdrawal is sent once, even if a button is pressed twice.

Custody

Where your money is held

Your CPAY account

Your CPAY balance is held by CPAY. CPAY records every payment to your links and every withdrawal, and you withdraw the balance as USDT to an address you save.

CPAY Wallet, a separate app

CPAY Wallet, when it is released, is a separate app: its keys stay on your phone and CPAY cannot move or recover money in it. Your CPAY balance is not kept in that app, and nothing moves between the two.
