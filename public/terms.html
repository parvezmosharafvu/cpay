2. How payments reach you

CPAY is payment software for the Bitcoin Lightning Network. Depending on how your account is set up, a customer's payment through a CPAY link either goes directly to a wallet you control (direct payments), or CPAY receives it in its platform wallet and deducts the disclosed CPAY fee. What happens to the rest depends on the Auto withdraw setting that applies to your account, which your merchant desk shows:

  • Auto withdraw on: CPAY forwards the rest as soon as it can to the payout address you saved: a Lightning address, or a USDT address on a supported network.
  • Auto withdraw off: CPAY adds the rest to your CPAY balance. You withdraw it yourself from the desk, to a Lightning address or USDT address you saved or to a Lightning invoice you provide.

CPAY sets Auto withdraw for the platform and may set it differently for an account; a change applies to payments made after it. When Auto withdraw is turned off for your account, forwards that were still waiting are added to your balance. For USDT, payments smaller than the network's minimum send are grouped and forwarded together once they reach it.

  • With Auto withdraw off, CPAY holds the amount in your balance until you withdraw it. Saving or changing a payout address needs your passkey; the new address is used at once. With Auto withdraw on, CPAY aims to forward each payment promptly but does not guarantee a time. A forward can be delayed or held, for example when no usable payout address is saved, when the amount is below the network minimum, when it is above a forwarding limit set by CPAY, or when a network or provider fails. A held amount is recorded as owed to you and is forwarded later or paid out to you; it is not lost. If your account cannot receive payments (for example it has no usable payout address or is not active), your links may refuse new payments. While a payment waits to be forwarded it is held in CPAY's platform wallet together with other users' payments; CPAY records each payment separately and does not invest or lend them.
  • CPAY does not have the keys to your wallet and cannot move, freeze, reverse or recover money in it.
  • Nothing in the Service is a deposit, an investment or insurance, and CPAY does not exchange currencies for you.
  • Amounts shown in US dollars are a price reference at the moment an invoice is created. The payment itself is made in bitcoin.
