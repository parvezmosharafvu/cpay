CPAY Wallet

A Lightning wallet for your own phone. Its keys are made and kept on your phone, so nobody can move the money in it without your phone's signature. CPAY cannot see, move or recover it. Payments run on our payment provider's network, where independent network operators co-sign each transfer, so payments depend on that service while it runs.

It is separate from your CPAY account. Your CPAY balance, deposits, earnings and USDT withdrawals stay on the website and work as before. Nothing moves between the two on its own.

What you need

  • Android phone: a current version of Chrome.
  • iPhone: iOS 16.4 or later, with Safari.
  • An internet connection to send and receive. The wallet opens and unlocks without one.
  • Paper and a pen for your 12-word recovery phrase.

Install

CPAY Wallet installs from the browser. There is no app store download and no APK file.

Android (Chrome)

  1. Tap Open CPAY Wallet above.
  2. Tap the menu (three dots), then Install app or Add to Home screen.
  3. Open it from the new icon on your home screen.

iPhone (Safari)

  1. Tap Open CPAY Wallet above, in Safari.
  2. Tap the Share button, then Add to Home Screen.
  3. Open it from the new icon. Use it from the icon, not from a Safari tab, so the phone keeps its data.

Security and recovery

  • When you create the wallet it shows 12 words. Write them on paper, in order, and keep the paper safe. They are the only way to recover the wallet if your phone is lost, broken or reset.
  • Anyone with the 12 words can take the money. CPAY will never ask for them. Never type them into a website, a form, a chat or an email.
  • The wallet asks for its own passphrase to unlock on your phone. Do not reuse your CPAY password.
  • To recover on a new phone, open CPAY Wallet there, choose Restore with a recovery phrase and type the 12 words.
  • Linking your CPAY account inside the wallet is optional. It only shows your CPAY balance there, labelled as held by CPAY. It never shares the 12 words, and you can unlink at any time.
