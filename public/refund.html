Refund and Dispute Policy

Last updated: [Effective date]. Operated by [Company legal name], [Registered address].

Payments are final on the network

A Lightning payment cannot be reversed or charged back by CPAY, Cash App or the payer's wallet once it completes. CPAY does not hold card details and cannot start a chargeback.

If you paid someone through a CPAY link

  • Contact the person or business you paid first. They are responsible for the goods or services they sell.
  • If they agree to a refund, they send it to you directly, for example from their own wallet. CPAY does not move money from their balance to you. [Reviewer: decide whether CPAY should offer a merchant-initiated refund tool.]
  • If you cannot resolve it, report it through the complaints procedure with the payment link and the time you paid. CPAY can confirm whether the payment arrived and can act under the Terms against an account used for fraud.

Invoice problems

  • Expired invoice: no money moves if an invoice expires before it is paid. Ask for a new link.
  • Paid but not shown: CPAY checks for payments that arrive late and settles them automatically. If a payment still does not appear, report it with the payment time and wallet.

Withdrawals

A USDT withdrawal sent to the address saved on your account cannot be recalled. If a withdrawal fails before it is sent, the amount returns to your balance. [Reviewer: liability for a wrong address entered by the account holder.]
