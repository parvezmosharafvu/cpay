Your own wallet

CPAY Wallet keeps its keys on this phone. CPAY cannot see, move or recover the money in it. It is separate from your CPAY website account and its balance.

  • Payments run on the Spark network. Each transfer needs your phone's signature and co-signing by Spark operators (two of Lightspark, Breez and Flashnet). They cannot move your money without you, but while they are working, payments depend on them. How this works
  • You get a 12-word recovery phrase. Write it on paper. It is the only way back in if this phone is lost or reset.
  • Never type it into a website, a chat or an email. CPAY staff will never ask for it.
